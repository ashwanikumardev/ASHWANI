50 Important General Knowledge Facts About India for Competitive Exams

📅 September 26, 2026✍️ Insight Hub⏱ 10 min read

Whether you are preparing for government competitive examinations (UPSC, SSC CGL, State PSCs, Banking), university entrance tests, or college trivia quizzes, having a structured command over Indian general knowledge is essential.

Here is an authoritative compilation of 50 high-yield general knowledge facts about India categorized into Constitution & Polity, National Symbols, Geography Superlatives, History, and Science.

1. Constitution and Polity Facts

Topic Core Fact Key Detail / Article
Longest Written Constitution The Constitution of India is the world's lengthiest written constitution. Originally had 395 articles in 22 parts and 8 schedules; today contains 448+ articles.
Drafting Committee Chairman Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Regarded as the "Father of the Indian Constitution."
Time Taken to Draft 2 Years, 11 Months, and 18 Days Adopted on November 26, 1949; came into effect on January 26, 1950 (Republic Day).
Preamble Philosophy "Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic, Republic" Words "Socialist", "Secular", and "Integrity" added via the 42nd Amendment Act (1976).
Calligrapher Prem Behari Narain Raizada Handwrote the original Constitution in flowing italic style without taking payment.

2. Official National Symbols of India

  1. National Emblem: The Lion Capital of Ashoka from Sarnath, featuring four Asiatic lions standing back-to-back, with the motto "Satyameva Jayate" (Truth Alone Triumphs) taken from the Mundaka Upanishad in Devanagari script.
  2. National Flag (Tiranga): Designed by freedom fighter Pingali Venkayya; adopted on July 22, 1947. Width-to-length ratio is strictly 2:3.
  3. National Anthem: "Jana Gana Mana", composed in Sanskritized Bengali by Rabindranath Tagore; playing time is precisely 52 seconds.
  4. National Song: "Vande Mataram", composed in Sanskrit by Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay in his 1882 novel Anandamath.
  5. National Aquatic Animal: The Ganges River Dolphin (Platanista gangetica), an endangered freshwater cetacean.
  6. National Heritage Animal: The Indian Elephant (declared in 2010 to boost conservation).
  7. National River: The Ganga (declared in November 2008).

4. Historic Firsts and Nobel Laureates

  1. First Indian Nobel Laureate: Rabindranath Tagore won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1913 for his poetry collection Gitanjali, becoming the first non-European to win a Nobel.
  2. First Science Nobel in Asia: Sir C.V. Raman won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1930 for the discovery of the Raman Effect (scattering of light), celebrated annually on February 28 as National Science Day.
  3. First President of India: Dr. Rajendra Prasad (served from 1950 to 1962).
  4. First Indian in Space: Wing Commander Rakesh Sharma flew aboard Soyuz T-11 on April 3, 1984. When Prime Minister Indira Gandhi asked how India looked from space, he famously answered: "Saare jahan se achha" (Better than the whole world).
  5. First Woman Prime Minister: Indira Gandhi (assumed office in January 1966).
  6. First Woman President: Pratibha Patil (served 2007–2012).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the southern-most point of Indian territory?+
Indira Point, located in Great Nicobar Island (6°45' N latitude), is the southern-most point of the Republic of India. The southernmost point of mainland India is Cape Comorin (Kanyakumari) in Tamil Nadu.
What is the Standard Meridian of India?+
The Standard Meridian of India is 82°30' East longitude, passing through Mirzapur near Prayagraj in Uttar Pradesh. Indian Standard Time (IST) is 5 hours and 30 minutes ahead of Greenwich Mean Time (UTC+05:30).
